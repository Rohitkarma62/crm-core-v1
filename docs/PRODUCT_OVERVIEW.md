# CRM Core V1 — Product Overview

## What is CRM Core?

CRM Core is a multi-tenant customer relationship management platform designed to give businesses one operational workspace for their customer lifecycle.

It connects the main stages of a business workflow:

**Lead → Pipeline → Follow-up → Customer → Sale → Payment → Reporting**

## Who is it for?

CRM Core is designed as a flexible foundation for:

- Service businesses
- Sales teams
- Agencies
- Local businesses
- Small and medium businesses
- Organizations that currently depend on spreadsheets for customer tracking

## The problem

Businesses often manage customer information across spreadsheets, messaging apps and disconnected notes.

That creates common operational problems:

- Leads get forgotten
- Follow-ups are missed
- Sales data is scattered
- Customer information becomes difficult to search
- Managers lack a single performance view
- Importing existing data becomes painful

CRM Core brings these workflows into one structured application.

## Product principles

### 1. One customer lifecycle

Customer data should move through a consistent workflow instead of being copied between tools.

### 2. Business isolation

Each business should only access its own data.

### 3. API-first architecture

The backend is exposed through a structured REST API so additional clients and integrations can be built later.

### 4. Modular growth

V1 establishes the core CRM domain. V2 can add automation, notifications, billing, richer analytics and integrations without rebuilding the foundation.

## V1 capabilities

- Authentication
- Business accounts
- Users and roles
- Lead management
- Pipeline
- Follow-ups
- Customers
- Sales
- Payments
- Dashboard analytics
- Reports
- CSV/XLSX import
- Import history
- Error reports
- CSV/XLSX export
- Tenant isolation
- Permission enforcement

## Product direction

CRM Core is being developed as a SaaS-ready CRM foundation.

V1 focuses on getting the operational CRM loop correct.

V2 is intended to add automation, richer analytics, notifications, integrations and SaaS-oriented capabilities.
